Notice from the range

Bragging rights:
temporarily suspended

Someone just sent you a Targetfall score. Impressive, probably. Unverifiable, definitely.

Targetfall isn't out yet. It's in closed testing, which means the person who sent you this can shoot arrows and you cannot. We appreciate that this is difficult. We ask you to bear it with dignity.

In the meantime, observe proper sportsmanship

  • Congratulate them. Out loud. Using words.
  • Do not say you "would've gotten more."
  • Do not question the accuracy percentage. It is calculated by a computer and you are not.
  • Do not ask whether they used their Rewind. Every run gets exactly one absorbed miss, and whether they spent it is between them and the range.
  • Do not point out that the second miss is the one that ends it, and therefore luck is involved.
    It is. Say nothing.

Full bragging rights — including the right to reply with a larger number — will be restored when the game launches on Google Play.

Until then, the scoreboard stands unchallenged, which is exactly how they wanted it.
